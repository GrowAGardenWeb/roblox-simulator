Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MoneyScripts (moneyscripts.shop) sells entertainment/novelty digital products — all of them are disclosed, fake "LARP" simulators (see the warning on every product). This privacy policy is about something different and completely real: the account and payment data we actually collect to run the store itself.

Who we are

MoneyScripts is an independently operated storefront at moneyscripts.shop. Contact: munfuja11@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Google account info, when you sign in: your email address, name, and profile picture, via Google Sign-In. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Payment info, when you buy something: handled entirely by Stripe. We do not receive or store your card number — only that a payment succeeded, for which product, and the email/name Stripe associates with it.
  • Purchase records: which product(s) your Google account has access to, so we know what you've paid for.

How we use it

  • To verify who you are and grant access to the product(s) you purchased.
  • To process payments through Stripe.
  • To respond if you contact us for support.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Google — for sign-in (Google's own privacy policy applies to their part of this).
  • Stripe — for payment processing (Stripe's own privacy policy applies to their part of this).
  • Cloudflare — our hosting/backend infrastructure provider, which stores the records described above.

We don't share your data with anyone else.

Data retention

Sign-in sessions expire automatically after 30 days. Purchase/access records are kept as long as your account may need access to what you bought. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Contact below) — we'll remove it unless we're required to keep payment records for legal/tax reasons.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we have about you, correct it, or delete it, by emailing munfuja11@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

MoneyScripts is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
